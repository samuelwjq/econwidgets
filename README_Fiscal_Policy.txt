FISCAL POLICY - G AND TAXES
Extract all 12 files into the repository root. Open Fiscal_Policy.html.
Index is unchanged.
Choose Government Expenditure (G) or Taxes (T), then Decrease / 0 / Increase.
G rises or T falls: expansionary; G falls or T rises: contractionary.
Summary strip identifies the fiscal stance automatically.
Taxes use an illustrative 60% initial consumption response; this is not an
empirical calibration. The multiplier then adds induced-spending effects.
No Further Rounds retains the initial effect. Small is the default multiplier.
The multiplier dropdown is compact. Optional purple spending stages and red
shift arrows remain available under Diagram Options, off by default.
Long-run capacity is optional and begins at 0. As a teaching assumption,
expansionary policy permits only capacity increases, contractionary only
capacity decreases, and neutral policy no capacity change. Instrument or
stance changes reset capacity to 0. The explanation is below the diagram.
This restriction is not a universal economic claim. AD is independent of AS.
Other autonomous spending, tax supply incentives, financing, crowding out and
time lags are not simulated. Export/reset/fullscreen/glossary use suite tools.
Validation: syntax, 270 model combinations, browser G/T direction mapping,
capacity restrictions and stance-change reset passed. Full responsive, touch,
native fullscreen and clipboard testing remain outstanding.
