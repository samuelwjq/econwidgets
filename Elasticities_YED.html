Glossary

Normal Goods

Normal goods refer to those goods whose demand increases when income increases.

Income Elasticity Of Demand

Income elasticity of demand (YED) measures the degree of responsiveness of demand for a good to a change in income, ceteris paribus.

Inferior Goods

Goods with negative YED are known as inferior goods.

Normal Goods (Necessities)

Goods with an income inelastic demand where 0 < YED < 1, i.e. a proportionate change in income leads to a less than proportionate change in demand, ceteris paribus (known as normal necessities).

Normal Goods (Luxury)

Goods with an income elastic demand where YED > 1, i.e. a proportionate change in income leads to a more than proportionate change in demand, ceteris paribus (known as luxury goods).

Zero Income Elasticity

If the YED value is equal to 0, demand has zero income elasticity, i.e. a change in income leads to no change in demand, ceteris paribus.

Demand

Refers to the entire relationship between price and quantity demanded, as represented by the entire demand curve (or the quantity of a good or service which consumers are willing and able to buy at different price levels, over a specific time period, ceteris paribus).

Supply

Refers to the entire relationship between price and quantity supplied, as represented by the entire supply curve.

Market Equilibrium

Occurs when quantity demanded equals quantity supplied (Qdd = Qss), resulting in a situation where there is no tendency for consumers and producers to change their willingness to buy and sell.
