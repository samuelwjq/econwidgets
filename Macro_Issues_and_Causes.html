Glossary

Aggregate Demand (AD)

Total planned expenditure on domestic final goods and services at each general price level. AD = C + I + G + (X − M).

Aggregate Supply (AS)

Total real output producers are willing and able to supply at each general price level over a period of time. The Keynesian curve has horizontal, intermediate and vertical sections.

Macroeconomic Equilibrium

The intersection of AD and AS determines the equilibrium general price level and real national income.

General Price Level (GPL)

The average level of prices of goods and services in the economy.

Real National Income (RNY)

The value of the economy’s output adjusted for price changes.

Productive Capacity

The output an economy can produce when its resources are fully employed. The vertical section of AS marks this level.

Actual and Potential Growth

Actual growth is an increase in real output. Potential growth is an increase in productive capacity. Negative actual growth is a fall in real output.

Inflation and Deflation

Inflation is a sustained rise in GPL. Deflation is a sustained fall in GPL.

Demand-Deficient Unemployment

Unemployment caused by insufficient AD. Firms produce less and require fewer workers.

Negative Output Gap

The amount by which actual real output is below potential output.
