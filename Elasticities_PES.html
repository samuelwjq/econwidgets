Glossary

Perfectly Price Inelastic Supply

PES = 0. Quantity supplied does not respond to a change in the good’s own price, ceteris paribus. The curve is vertical.

Perfectly Price Elastic Supply

PES approaches infinity. The curve is horizontal: any amount can be supplied at the given price. An arbitrarily small price change causes an unbounded proportional quantity response in this limiting case, ceteris paribus.

Price Elasticity Of Supply

The price elasticity of supply (PES) measures the degree of responsiveness of quantity supplied of a good to a change in the price of the good itself, ceteris paribus.

Price Inelastic Supply

If the PES value is less than 1, the supply for the good is price inelastic, i.e. a proportionate change in price leads to a less than proportionate change in quantity supplied, ceteris paribus.

Unit Elastic Supply

If the PES value is equal to 1, the supply for the good is unitary price elastic, i.e. a percentage change in price is equal in size to the percentage change in quantity supplied.

Price Elastic Supply

If the PES value is greater than 1, supply for the good is price elastic, i.e. a proportionate change in price leads to a more than proportionate change in quantity supplied, ceteris paribus.

Total Revenue/Expenditure

Total Revenue (firms) = Price (P) × Quantity (Q) = Total Expenditure (consumers).

Price Effect

The gain or loss in total revenue/expenditure from the price change on the initial quantity Q0.

Price Effect = Q0 × (P1 − P0).

Quantity Effect

The gain or loss in total revenue/expenditure from the quantity change valued at the new price P1.

Quantity Effect = P1 × (Q1 − Q0).

Demand

Refers to the entire relationship between price and quantity demanded, as represented by the entire demand curve (or the quantity of a good or service which consumers are willing and able to buy at different price levels, over a specific time period, ceteris paribus).

Supply

Refers to the entire relationship between price and quantity supplied, as represented by the entire supply curve.

Market Equilibrium

Occurs when quantity demanded equals quantity supplied (Qdd = Qss), resulting in a situation where there is no tendency for consumers and producers to change their willingness to buy and sell.
